FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we switch plans as we grow?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at the start of any billing cycle — most clients start with Starter or Professional and move to Enterprise as automation scope expands.

Is there a setup fee?

Onboarding and ERP integration work is scoped and quoted separately based on system complexity, and shared transparently before you commit.

What counts as a 'transaction'?

A transaction is any invoice, bill, payroll entry or bank line processed through our automation — most SMEs comfortably fit within Starter or Professional tiers.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes, annual billing saves 20% versus monthly across every plan, including Custom Solutions.

Not sure which plan fits?

Book a free consultation and we'll recommend the right starting point based on your transaction volume and goals.
