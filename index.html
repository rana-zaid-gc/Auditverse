FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What makes AuditVerse AI different from a typical ERP consultancy?

Every engagement starts with a Chartered Accountant-led diagnostic of your controls and processes, not a software checklist. Automation is then built around what actually reduces risk and manual effort for your business.

Can you work with our existing ERP or accounting software?

Yes. We integrate with Odoo, SAP, Oracle NetSuite, QuickBooks, Zoho and most major platforms, and can also automate around legacy or custom systems using API and RPA connectors.

How long does a typical automation project take?

Most finance and accounting automation projects go live within 6–10 weeks. Full ERP transformations typically run 3–6 months depending on scope and number of entities.

Do you provide ongoing support after go-live?

Every plan includes a named engagement manager and 24/7 support desk. Enterprise clients also receive a quarterly controls and automation health review.
