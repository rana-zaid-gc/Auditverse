ERP Automation: one connected system instead of twelve spreadsheets

We implement, integrate and automate Odoo, SAP Business One and Oracle NetSuite — turning your ERP from a data-entry chore into a self-updating source of truth.

Implementation + Automation

ERP that updates itself, not one your team has to feed

A traditional ERP rollout stops at 'go-live' — your team still keys in every invoice, every stock movement, every payroll entry by hand. We go further: every module we implement ships with the AI Bots that keep it running with minimal manual touch.

Whether you're standing up Odoo from scratch or automating an existing SAP or NetSuite instance, our approach is the same — map the process, design the control, automate the repetition.

  • Pre-built connectors for invoice, payroll and procurement automation
  • Multi-entity, multi-currency and multi-warehouse configurations

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current ERP entirely?

Not always. In many engagements we automate around your existing system using API and RPA bridges before recommending a full migration.

How disruptive is an ERP migration?

We run parallel environments and phase cutovers by module or entity, so day-to-day operations continue uninterrupted.

Can you support multi-country operations?

Yes — multi-entity, multi-currency and localised tax configuration (including VAT and ZATCA) is standard in our implementations.
