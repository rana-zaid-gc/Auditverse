Enterprise Software, Not Scripts

Automation built like software, governed like an audit

Most 'automation' projects are brittle scripts that break the moment a vendor changes an invoice template. We build AI Bots as enterprise software — versioned, monitored, and reviewed by Chartered Accountants for control integrity.

Every bot logs what it did, why, and what exception it raised — so your external auditors trust the automation as much as your finance team does.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will AI bots replace our finance team?

No — bots absorb repetitive, rules-based work so your team can focus on analysis, judgment calls and stakeholder decisions. Every engagement includes upskilling your staff to supervise the automation.

How do you prevent automation errors from compounding?

Every bot includes confidence thresholds — transactions below the threshold are routed to a human reviewer instead of being auto-posted, and every action is logged for audit.

Can bots work across multiple entities or currencies?

Yes, our bots are built multi-entity and multi-currency from the ground up, a common requirement for our logistics and trading clients.
