Madina Group

Automated Invoice-to-Pay Across the Group

Madina Group (Madina Private Limited) automated 3-way invoice matching and vendor payment approvals across its business units, replacing a fully manual, paper-heavy process.

  • 80% faster invoice processing
  • Duplicate and erroneous payments virtually eliminated
  • Finance staff redeployed from data entry to vendor management
Galaxy Broadcasting · 92 News

Real-Time Financial Dashboards for a Media House

Galaxy Broadcasting, operator of 92 News, replaced weekly manual finance reports with live Power BI dashboards covering revenue, production costs and cash position.

  • Leadership sees cash and revenue position in real time
  • Monthly reporting prep time cut from days to hours
  • Department-level budget variance now tracked automatically
Sayyed Engineers

Project Costing Automation for an Engineering Firm

Sayyed Engineers automated project-level costing and subcontractor payment tracking, giving management visibility into project margins as work progressed rather than after completion.

  • Project margin visibility available mid-project, not at close-out
  • Subcontractor payment cycle time reduced significantly
  • Consolidated reporting across concurrent projects
Brain Telecommunications Pvt Limited

Payroll & HR Workflow Automation

Brain Telecommunications automated payroll processing, attendance reconciliation and statutory filings across its workforce, removing manual payroll calculation errors.

  • 65% reduction in manual HR and payroll work
  • Payroll cycle shortened with zero statutory filing delays
  • Attendance and leave data now flows directly into payroll
Faisalabad Oil Refinery

Continuous Monitoring for a Manufacturing Operation

Faisalabad Oil Refinery deployed continuous AI-powered transaction monitoring across procurement and inventory, replacing periodic manual sampling with always-on control checks.

  • Full transaction coverage versus a small manual sample
  • Control gaps identified and closed mid-year
  • Procurement approval cycle significantly faster
Kashmir Sugar Mills

ERP Automation for Seasonal Production Accounting

Kashmir Sugar Mills implemented automated production costing and inventory reconciliation to handle the sharp seasonal swings typical of sugar milling operations.

  • Inventory reconciliation automated across crushing season
  • Faster, more accurate cost-per-tonne reporting
  • Reduced manual reconciliation workload during peak season
